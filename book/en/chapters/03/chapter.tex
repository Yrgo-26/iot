\lecture{Lecture 3}{A protocol of your own}

{\large\itshape Several nodes on one shared wire, and everything that is needed when it is not
perfect.\par}

\begin{chapterbox}{In this chapter}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The shared bus, and why broadcast is the norm in embedded systems.
  \item Routing on DST and SRC, and why it sits in the node and not in the bus.
  \item An error model that is \emph{deterministic}, and why that is the whole point.
  \item ACK and NACK, timeout, retransmission --- and the duplicates that follow.
\end{itemize}
\end{chapterbox}
