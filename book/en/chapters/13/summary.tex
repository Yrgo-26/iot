\section*{Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Summary}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \code{VDDIO2} and a common ground first. Without them a correct SPI implementation looks
    like a broken one.
  \item The FPGA node's single-wire bus becomes a real differential CAN bus through a transceiver,
    whose transmit input is to be \textbf{low for dominant}. Terminate with 120~\textohm{} at each
    end.
  \item Climb the ladder in order, and \textbf{never have more than one unknown at a time}.
  \item Rung 1 (the loopback) is the transport's only test --- that class is covered by no
    automated test.
  \item Rung 2 (the masking to eleven bits) proves that you are talking to the register bank and
    not to an echo of your own wire.
  \item Debug with hypotheses and measurements, not by swapping wires. Suspect your own half
    first, prove it before you report it, change one thing at a time.
\end{itemize}
